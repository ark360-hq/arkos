# ADR-0003: Apache 2.0 Licence

**Status:** Accepted

**Date:** 2026-05-13

**Supersedes:** (none)

**Superseded by:** (none)

---

## Context

ARK OS is open-source IP from the ARK360 practice. The licence must maximise adoption while protecting ARK360's attribution rights. The candidate licences considered were MIT, Apache 2.0, and EUPL 1.2. MIT offers no patent protection. EUPL is copyleft and would restrict commercial adoption. Apache 2.0 grants patent peace, permits commercial use, and requires downstream notices.

## Decision

ARK OS is licenced under Apache 2.0. All derivative works must include the Apache 2.0 licence text and retain attribution notices referencing ARK360.

## Consequences

Plus: Apache 2.0 is acceptable to enterprise legal teams and procurement reviewers. Patent peace clause reduces legal risk for adopters.

Plus: The attribution requirement means ARK360 is credited in derivative works, supporting the practice's reputation and authority.

Minus: Apache 2.0 does not prevent a competitor from shipping a commercial product based on ARK OS without contributing back. This is mitigated by the attribution requirement and ARK360's practice reputation as the origin of the framework.
